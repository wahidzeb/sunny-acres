How to play

🌱 Tap an empty field and pick a seed to plant it. Keep your finger down and swipe over more fields to plant them all!

🌾 When crops are ready they bounce. Tap or swipe over them to harvest. Each field gives 2 crops.

🐔 Tap an animal pen to feed the animals, then tap again to collect eggs, milk and wool.

🍞 Tap a building like the Bakery to make bread, cakes, cheese and more.

📋 Fill truck orders for coins and stars. Level up to unlock new crops, animals and buildings.

👆 Drag the farm to look around. Everything keeps growing while you are away!
