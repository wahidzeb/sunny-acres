About Sunny Acres

Sunny Acres is a cosy farm game for kids and grown-ups. You start with a few fields and 40 coins, plant wheat, fill truck orders for friendly villagers, and slowly grow into a farm with animals, a bakery and a loom.

There are 6 crops, animals that each eat a different crop, and buildings like the Bakery, Dairy, Kitchen and Loom that turn produce into goodies.

3 quick tips

  1. Swipe, do not tap. Pick a seed once and swipe over every empty field.
  2. Keep feed in the barn. Chickens need wheat, cows need corn and sheep need carrots.
  3. Check truck orders before selling. An order usually pays far more than the barn.

How to play

🌱 Tap an empty field and pick a seed to plant it. Keep your finger down and swipe over more fields to plant them all!

🌾 When crops are ready they bounce. Tap or swipe over them to harvest. Each field gives 2 crops.

🐔 Tap an animal pen to feed the animals, then tap again to collect eggs, milk and wool.

🍞 Tap a building like the Bakery to make bread, cakes, cheese and more.

📋 Fill truck orders for coins and stars. Level up to unlock new crops, animals and buildings.

👆 Drag the farm to look around. Everything keeps growing while you are away!
